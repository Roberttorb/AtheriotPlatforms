About Atheriot Platforms

Small Software.
Meaningful impact.

Atheriot Platforms is an independent software initiative driven by a simple belief: technology should make everyday work easier, smarter, and more efficient. What began as a solution to real-world challenges, such as tracking technician travel expenses and calculating overtime earnings, is evolving into a growing ecosystem of practical, purpose-built software.

Today, our focus is on refining Atheriot Travel and Atheriot Overtime while expanding our vision through Atheriot Development — our dedicated web development and digital solutions division. From professional business websites to custom web applications, we transform ideas into modern, responsive, and reliable digital experiences tailored to your business needs.
